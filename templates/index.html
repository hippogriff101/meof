FAQ

What is Meof?

Meof is a Hack Club YSWS brought to you by the creators of Woof! We are combining two older YSWS's into one.

What is Stardance?

Stardance is a free online summer program for teens 13–18. Build any technical project you want — websites, games, apps, hardware — log your hours, and earn real prizes! Sponsored by NASA, AMD and GitHub.

What is Hackatime?

We use Hackatime to verify time coded! It is an editor extension that tracks the time you spend coding. You can install it here.

How do I participate?

You need to be 13 to 18 years old to participate! Your identity does need to be verified using Hack Clubs secure accounts portal. A Slack and Github account is also required as well as a demo link (or video for hardware), repo link, screenshot and hackatime project!

What can I build?

Anything to do with dogs or cats! It could be a website, an app, or even a hardware project.

Why is this a thing?

This sidequest comes from the people who made Woof a standalone YSWS that ran at the start of 2026. You spent 5 hours codinga dog themed project and get a dog plushie! Our sponsor, @AVD, also ran Meow which is the same consept but with cats!!
